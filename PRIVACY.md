# VYRO Privacy Policy (MVP template)

VYRO stores account/session data, profile information, videos, comments, likes and follows needed to operate the service. Uploaded media is processed by the configured Cloudflare Stream account. Authentication/session tokens are stored server-side and should never be embedded as Cloudflare credentials in the Android app.

Users should be able to request deletion of their account and associated content through the production support channel before store launch. This document is a technical MVP template, not legal advice; replace it with a reviewed policy and your actual legal/support contact before public release.
