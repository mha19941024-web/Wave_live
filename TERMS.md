# VYRO Terms of Service (MVP template)

VYRO is a user-generated-content platform. Production launch should prohibit unlawful content, harassment, impersonation, copyright infringement, fraud and abusive automation. The service should reserve the right to remove content and suspend accounts that violate the rules.

Before public launch, replace this template with reviewed terms, an abuse/reporting workflow, age requirements, moderation policy and a valid support/contact address.
